I am a **PhD Student** in Computer Science and an **Automotive Software Engineer**. My research theme is "Environment Perception for Intelligent Vehicles Motion Planning in Highly Dynamic Scenarios".

My current work focuses on closing the gap between efficient vectorized scene representations (e.g., *VAD / VADv2*) and continuous volumetric occupancy fields (e.g., *FlashOcc*) to enable safe navigation in the presence of Out-of-Distribution (OoD) obstacles and unstructured environments. Additionally, I am particluarly interested in real-time systems and in sensor fusion approaches to combine data from different sensor sources for efficient and comprehensive perception.

### 🔬 Research Interests
* **Perception:** 3D Occupancy Networks, Semantic Voxel Grids, BEV Feature Extraction, Multi-Camera Sensor Fusion.
* **Planning & Control:** Differentiable Collision Costs, Model Predictive Control (MPC), Risk-Aware & Probabilistic Trajectory Planning.
* **Autonomous Systems:** End-to-End Autonomous Driving architectures and Planning-Oriented Autonomous Driving.
